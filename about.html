We find where operations leak money — and we get rid of it. Now with AI.

Voorsprong is led by Marten, a mechanical engineer with a passion — and a long track record — for finding inefficiencies and getting rid of them. A filter redesign that saved an energy company $300,000 a year. A saw insert that took $60k a year of waste out of a timber plant. A valve that handed a crew back hours a day. Then he built and ran More Circular, a manufacturing business, almost single-handed for ten years — production, R&D, systems, website, marketing, admin, all of it.

So we know exactly what it feels like when the operation itself is the bottleneck. And over the last few months we've rebuilt More Circular to run on AI — a live command centre for the numbers, a configurator that lifts conversion, automated ad monitoring, hands-off financial oversight. The same engineer's eye for waste, now paired with tools that strip out the repetitive work.

That's the combination we bring: people who've actually run the machine and can build the fix. No jargon, no year-long transformation — one conversation tells us whether there's real money worth going after, and what the return would look like.
