Two tools

A quick look — built around the Coolstore 45 job. Best on a laptop.

1 · Quote Builder

From the client's spec to a quote

Starts from the client's specification. The engineer designs the install and does a basic P&ID; the tool reads it and takes out a lot of the manual work — the pricing inputs and the emails to suppliers. Made to help the engineer build the quote, saving a lot of repetitive work.

Open the Quote Builder →
2 · Margin Tracker

Track the job as it runs

Follows the project while it's live. You just import the latest Accredo data and it works out the variance for each category and the overall budget vs actual automatically — for sticking to budget, seeing where the costs are going, and protecting the margin.
