Equipment schedule — all 3 coolstores

  • 750 kW glycol chilling plant
  • 36 FCUs (EC, ≥30kW, 3 fans, 6mm alu fins, warm-glycol defrost)
  • 20 humidifiers (Carel Humidisk 6.4 F2070)
  • 18 return-air monitoring stations
  • 10 ethylene flushing fans (Elta Compact 2000)
  • 10 zone control stations
  • 6 X-sense · 12 cameras · 20 data APs
  • 4 forklift + 6 shuttle charging stations
  • 3 "man-on-foot" sensor/warning systems
CS45 rooms: 2× FCU (45-D) / 4× per room (45-A/B/C), central apex, opposing pairs · 8 Humidisk · temp/humidity/low-temp probes per FCU pair · LEDs >150 lux, battens parallel to airflow.

Scope of work

  • Detailed design, 2 reviews (75% Developed, 100% IFC)
  • Refrigeration plant (compressors, condensers, HX) in dedicated plant room
  • Glycol system: pumps, headers, SS ring mains, tie-in to 42-44
  • FCUs, humidification, valve stations, sensors
  • Pipework + insulation (SS, seismic restraints)
  • Electrical: 750 kVA transformer, MSB, MCC, VSDs, cabling
  • PLC / HMI / controls (match 42-44, integrate Intech), power-quality
  • HAZOP (if ammonia), commissioning, 2-wk UAT, training, O&M, as-builts

Exclusions

  • Fire-alarm install & wiring Pioneer
  • Concrete pads / foundations Civil
  • Racking & ops equipment · power-retailer metering · builder's temp power
  • MCC room construction (if prefab not available)

Nominated parties

IT/Data · Vision LabCivils · WilliamsonsPM · TEG + S&LFire · Pioneer

Site & existing conditions

  • Existing Coolstore 42-44 (2022) = the template
  • Existing glycol reserves — compatible, allow mixing
  • 11kV power; 1-/3-phase at CS44
  • Integrate existing Intech control system
  • Laydown adjacent CS45; CS44C for assembly/fab; portacom office

Constraints & standards

  • Ammonia → HAZOP + AS/NZS 2022 + emergency response
  • Fire: min 60-min materials; Vulcan FE design
  • Insulation: PIR / PU inj, 22g stucco alu clad, 15-yr — not Armaflex
  • Pipe: SS >50mm = 316; ASTM 106B (>−33°C) / 333 (<−33°C)
  • Labelling: engraved Traffolyte — Dymotape not approved
  • VSDs: ABB/Rockwell/Schneider/Danfoss · THD <5%

Deliverables to price

  • Method · org chart · project examples · QA · timeline
  • Recommended spares list with prices
  • 12-month maintenance agreement
  • As-builts · O&M (2 bound + electronic) · commissioning & quality plans

Commercials & drawings

  • Lump sum ex-GST · milestone / monthly progress claims
